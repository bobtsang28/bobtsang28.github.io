Hello there! My name is Bob. I was raised in Hong Kong before moving to the UK to pursue a career in film and television production.

My passion for filmmaking stems from my background in mountaineering. I began filming and editing travel videos years ago to document my experiences climbing mountains around the world. Since then, I’ve completed the Seven Summits, reaching the highest peak on every continent, including Mount Everest, and have gone on to work across feature films, television dramas, mini-documentaries and other productions.

Now based in London, I work as a freelance filmmaker, camera assistant, photographer, editor and colourist, with experience in remote, extreme and physically demanding environments. Alongside client work, I develop short films and mini-documentaries as personal projects, exploring the relationship between people and nature, and how memory and cultural identity shape individuals and their pursuit of personal aspirations.

My cinematographic approach emphasises naturalistic imagery and restrained camera movement. I favour wide, deliberate framing, natural lighting, earthy tones and organic textures, allowing moments to unfold naturally. Through an observational approach, I aim to create immersive and intimate imagery that captures the beauty of everyday life.

Available for shoots across the UK and internationally.
